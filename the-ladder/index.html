The Ladder

Some doors are built to be opened. This one is bolted to a wall

ATLAS VALLEY

Literary Science Fiction

One Saturday, an iron ladder appears on the Harlow family’s backyard retaining wall. It rises a single foot above the stone and leads nowhere.

Eight-year-old Velara climbs it anyway.

Her fathers, the brothers Elias and Declan Harlow, follow her over the top and into Vel Anor, a world of crystal towers, singing cliffs, and two moons beneath a binary sun. Elias is a linguist who reads seven languages, and not one of them is spoken here. Declan, a soldier by training, knows how to hold a line, but there is no line to hold.

With the help of Naevara, a Veth archivist who keeps the memory of an entire people, the brothers begin to learn the rules of a planet that has been expecting them. Every answer has a price. The ladder keeps its own schedule. And the road home may not lead back to the life they left.

Some doors only open for those who are needed.
